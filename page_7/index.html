A Fable About Happiness

The City Mouse
and the Country Mouse

chasing horns, towers, and the quiet taste of barley

Country
City

Once upon a time, there were two mice. One of them lived in the country, and the other lived in the city. One day, the city mouse paid a visit to his good friend in the country. He told the country mouse about life in the city with great passion. The country mouse was envious of city life.

🐭
City Mouse
Hey, buddy, this place is too outdated. You eat grains and barley every day. Even the ants in my place would not eat them. Look at your house. I don't even know how you could live here. Just come to the city with me for a visit. The cream cakes there are piled up as high as a mountain, and the houses reach the sky.
🐭
Country Mouse
Really? I can go with you to see the city?

The country mouse went to the city with the city mouse, who kept boasting about the wonderful life in the city on their way.

🐭
City Mouse
Did you know that people in the city all use cars? No one is willing to walk. There is so much delicious food in the city. You will love it.
🐭
Country Mouse
Um, I want to be in the city right now.

The two mice walked for a long time and finally arrived in the city.

The country mouse stared in awe at the tall buildings. He did not want to blink, in case he would miss some of these sights. Out of nowhere, a car zoomed by, almost running over them.

🐭
City Mouse
Um, the cars are coming! Run away!
🐭
Country Mouse
Huh? What is that monster?

After walking through many twisting roads and bending alleys, the city mouse and the country mouse finally arrived at a kitchen.

🐭
City Mouse
Look! This is a kitchen. There are cakes, roasted chickens, hams, cheese— you can eat whatever you want. I eat this every day.
🐭
Country Mouse
Wow, so much delicious food and my favorite cheese! You eat this every day? You must be so happy.
🐭
City Mouse
Come on, eat and enjoy yourself.
🐭
Country Mouse
Wow! Oh my! This is so delicious. The city is so great.
⛔ Danger

However, while the two mice were enjoying themselves, the cat guarding the kitchen noticed them and ran towards them.

🐭
City Mouse
Hey, hey! What are we running for?
🐭
Country Mouse
If we do not run, the cat will eat us, because that delicious food does not belong to us.

The two mice ran out of the kitchen. Before they could catch their breath, two dogs ran towards them.

🐭
Country Mouse
Yeah, why is the dog also chasing us? Is it also because of the food?
🐭
City Mouse
Those dogs do not like to eat us. What they like is to feed us up.

The two mice managed to lose the dog and escape down a street. However, when the people on the street saw them, they began to chase and hit them.

Oh my god! The stinking mice! Get out of here!
🐭
Country Mouse
Wow! The human beings are beating us as well. So this is your life in the city? Aren't you always being chased by the cat, the dog, or humans?
🐭
City Mouse
Today was nothing. Sometimes people put mouse traps near the door. If you touch one, you could lose your foot. Some people even place rat poison in the food.
🐭
Country Mouse
That's fine. Just avoid them.
🐭
City Mouse
How could they stop chasing us? We just go back to the food.
🐭
Country Mouse
But I do not want to live in fear every day. I do not want to worry about my life. Although the barley in the country is not as tasty as the cheese in the city, I can live a free and easy life. Goodbye. I'm going back to the country. You may enjoy the scary life in the city yourself.

Despite the fact that the city mouse kept persuading him to stay, the country mouse took his luggage and went back to the country. The city mouse thought sadly to itself,

Alas, was the life in the country really so good?

✦   The city glitters, but freedom and peace have a quiet taste of barley.   ✦
